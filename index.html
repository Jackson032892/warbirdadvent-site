FAQs

How does it work?
From the 1st of December, open one a night. Each one is a mini 3D aircraft on its own display stand, ready for a desk or shelf. By Christmas Eve, all 24 are lined up.
What's inside?
24 mini 3D aircraft, one for each night from 1 to 24 December, each on its own display stand: WWII fighters and bombers, jets, stealth bombers, transport planes and helicopters. They're small collectibles for a desk or shelf, not large die-cast models.
How big is it?
Each plane is about 4 cm (1.6 in) nose to tail and 2 cm (0.8 in) tall on its stand. The box is about 26 × 18 × 3 cm (10.2 × 7.1 × 1.2 in).
When will it arrive?
Shipping is free and every order is tracked. Your order leaves within 1 to 2 business days, then delivery takes 6 to 10 business days. Order by 14 November to open the first one on the 1st.
What if it arrives damaged or I change my mind?
If it arrives damaged or faulty, email us and we replace it or refund you in full, and you do not pay return shipping. If you change your mind, you have 30 days from delivery to send it back unopened for a refund.

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account.
